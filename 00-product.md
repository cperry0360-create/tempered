# 00 — Product

## One line

Real training data drives character progression.

## Why "Tempered"

Tempering strengthens metal through controlled stress followed by rest. Skip the rest
and the metal is brittle. That is the product thesis in one word, and it is why this app
rewards a logged rest day and refuses to punish a missed one.

## The loop

1. Train, sleep, eat, read. Live.
2. Log it — most of it measured, some of it marked.
3. Measured performance raises five attributes.
4. Attributes carry levels, tiers, rank and titles.
5. Once a day the character fights, passively, and brings back rewards.
6. Progression is visible, causal, and explains itself.

## Who it is for

Cory first, on an iPhone. Later, people who want RPG progression without a complicated
fitness app, a full mobile game, punitive streaks, or every behaviour turned into an
optimisation problem.

## Principles

1. **Tracker first.** Delete the RPG layer and a good tracker remains.
2. **Measured beats marked.** If it can be measured, it is not a checkbox.
3. **Legible mapping.** What you did explains what grew, in one sentence.
4. **No punishment.** Missing days produce no gain, never a loss.
5. **Rest is an action.** Recovery is logged and rewarded, not an absence.
6. **Generous caps.** Discourage grinding without making good days feel unrewarded.
7. **Local-first.** No backend required, ever, for the core product.
8. **Balance is data.** Numbers live in config and are tuned by simulation.
9. **Flavour in language, not decoration.** The fantasy is in the words.
10. **Nothing punishes the body.** Weight is recorded, never scored.

## What differentiates it

Competitors reward ticking "Strength training". This rewards adding five pounds to your
top set, and tells you which attribute that moved and why.
