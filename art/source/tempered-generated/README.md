# Recovered Tempered art sources

These are the original generated files recovered from Cory's shared Companion Art Plan
conversation on 2026-09-08. The chat asset URLs were temporary, so the originals are
kept here even when only an optimized production derivative is loaded by the app.

The numbered files preserve generation order. They cover:

- master, Today, Train, Companion/Progress background concepts;
- five companion growth stages;
- hydration, idle, exercise, reading, sleep, and growth animation sheets;
- starter, mid, and fully upgraded habitats;
- hats, interaction props, and habitat decor;
- the widget style board, empty card shells, and Nutrition, Body Metrics, Water, and
  Training Load widget references;
- AI Nutrition, Progress, and Companion icons.

Production-ready transparent originals are true RGBA PNGs. The files for young/adult/
ascended stages were also delivered with a visible gray checkerboard; cleaned transparent
production cutouts are stored separately as `art/tempered/companion-stage-3.png` through
`companion-stage-5.png`. Several unused animation and widget-reference images also retain
the checkerboard and must not be rendered directly.

One generated Companion background download was truncated after its temporary URL had
expired, so it is intentionally not committed. The complete starter/mid/full habitat
scenes cover that screen in production.
