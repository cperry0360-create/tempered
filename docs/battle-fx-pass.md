# Battle FX pass

Version 0.12.10 adds the approved pixel combat effects to the Daily Battle without changing battle math or rewards.

Manual actions:
- Attack: sword slash, then impact spark; critical hits use the critical flash.
- Guard: shield flash on the hero.
- Skill: purple magic burst; critical skills add the critical flash.
- Enemy retaliation: dust impact on the hero when the hit is not guarded.

The effects are presentation-only and are preloaded/cached for the PWA.
